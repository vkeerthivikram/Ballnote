# Ballnote

Native Android utility for team-only turf cricket scoring. The primary task is recording a delivery between balls, outdoors, on a phone. Common outcomes take one tap. Scores, results and team records derive from ordered deliveries and match rules.

V1 includes reusable teams, three extras modes, both innings, automatic saving, arbitrary delivery corrections with a confirmation preview, history, result image sharing, and manual JSON backup/restore. Everything runs locally. No accounts, server, analytics, advertisements or automatic backup. Android 8+; English; phone first.

The approved visual authority is the Material You prototype in the linked planning chat: blue tonal scoreboard, rounded large scoring keys, light/dark themes. Preserve this identity. Native Android accessibility, Back navigation and system insets take precedence over decoration.
